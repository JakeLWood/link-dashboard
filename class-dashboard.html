Intro to Data Analytics

A foundational course covering spreadsheet analysis in Excel, relational databases with MySQL, and core data cleaning practices — building the skills needed to explore, query, and communicate insights from real datasets.

👩‍🏫 Instructor: Dr. Maria Chen  •  office hours Tues/Thurs 2–3:30pm
📣 Welcome to the course!

This dashboard is your home base for the semester. Check the Syllabus tab for the unit schedule, Assignments for due dates and submission links, and Materials for readings and reference resources. New announcements will appear here throughout the term.

Quick overview

6 units · 6 assignments · weekly readings and hands-on exercises in Excel and MySQL, culminating in a final capstone data analysis project.

Syllabus

Course units and weekly schedule

📄 View full syllabus in Canvas (Simple Syllabus) →
Week 1
Unit 1: Data Fundamentals

Introduction to data types, structured vs. unstructured data, and the analytics workflow — from raw data to actionable insight.

Week 2–3
Unit 2: Excel Essentials

Core formulas (VLOOKUP, IF, SUMIFS), building pivot tables, and creating charts to summarize and visualize datasets.

Week 4
Unit 3: Intro to SQL & MySQL

Setting up MySQL, writing basic SELECT statements, filtering with WHERE, and sorting results.

Week 5–6
Unit 4: Joins & Aggregation

Combining tables with JOIN, grouping data with GROUP BY, and summarizing with aggregate functions (COUNT, SUM, AVG).

Week 7
Unit 5: Data Cleaning

Identifying and handling missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent formatting to prepare data for analysis.

Week 8
Unit 6: Capstone Project

Apply Excel and MySQL skills to analyze a real-world dataset and present findings in a final report.
